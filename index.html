Aug 2026 - Present

Research Assistant

NUS LinS Lab & RoboScience Lab · PI: Lin Shao · Shenzhen, China

WAQ: Unified World–Action–Critic Model

Sixth author · Planned submission
  • Owned the end-to-end experimental pipeline for four embodiments across five manipulation settings: data collection and curation, video–action SFT, offline RL critic refinement, deployment, evaluation, and demonstrations.
  • Performed video–action supervised fine-tuning followed by offline-RL critic refinement with the video–action backbone frozen, for xArm-gripper, bimanual xArm-gripper, xHand, and xHand with FingerEye.
  • In the reported xArm–xHand eggplant-to-cup evaluation, WAQ achieved 70% full-task success versus 20% for LingBot-VA.

CRL-VLA: Continual Vision–Language–Action Learning

Fourth author · Submitted to ICLR 2027
  • Owned the offline and online RL real-robot pipeline on xarm-gripper, from data collection and training to deployment and checkpoint-level evaluation; trained baseline and CRL-VLA policies.
  • Contributed to method and real-robot experiment design for continual VLA learning with a frozen retention critic and a trainable adaptation critic.
  • Five-task offline xArm evaluation: 58% mean final task success versus 34% for the baseline, and 82% versus 60% grasp success. These real-robot comparisons are descriptive single-run evaluations.
